01无广告Ad-free

广告？
根本不会出现。Ads?
They never show up.

广告卡片在数据解析的那一刻就被剔除，不会先闪现、再消失。竖屏视频和过短的视频，也可以一并隐去。看什么，由你决定。Ad cards are removed the moment the feed is parsed, so nothing flickers in and out. Vertical and very short videos can go too. What you see is up to you.

  • 数据，一点不收集。No data collected.不接入统计 SDK，也没有自己的服务器。No analytics SDKs. No servers of its own.
  • 登录信息，只留在本机。Your login stays put.凭据仅保存在 iPhone 的钥匙串中。Credentials live only in your iPhone's Keychain.

02流畅Smooth

快，
而且一直快。Fast.
And it stays fast.

NeoBili 汲取了 PiliPlus 等开源客户端在接口与播放上的经验，再用 iOS 原生的方式，把每一处细节重新实现一遍。NeoBili learns from open-source clients like PiliPlus on APIs and playback, then rebuilds every detail the native iOS way.

  • 原生列表承载A truly native feed 推荐流由 UICollectionView 承载，新内容只插入新行，屏幕上的卡片纹丝不动。The feed runs on UICollectionView. New pages only insert rows; cards on screen stay put.
  • 进屏之前就备好Ready before it's seen 封面按卡片尺寸解码，标题在后台排好版。卡片一出现，就是完整的。Covers are decoded to size and titles laid out in the background, so every card arrives complete.
  • 停稳再加载，点开即播放Load on pause. Play on tap. 在卡片上稍作停留才预取播放地址。一划而过不占网络，停下的那一张轻点就播。Streams are prefetched only when you linger. Cards you fly past cost nothing; the one you stop on plays instantly.
  • 弹幕只移动位置Danmaku that only moves 每条弹幕预先渲染并按内容缓存，每一帧只改坐标。轨道分配参考 PiliPlus，互不重叠。Each comment is pre-rendered and cached, so frames only move it. Lane rules follow PiliPlus, with no overlaps.
  • 直播，进来就看Live, the moment you enter 优先 FLV，不行再换 HLS；画面不等主播资料加载，弹幕也不挡首帧。FLV first, HLS as a fallback. Video never waits for the streamer's profile, and chat never blocks the first frame.
  • 首帧优先First frame first 视频弹幕在第一帧之后才加载，解析放在后台，点开就先看到画面。Video danmaku loads after the first frame and parses in the background, so the picture always comes first.
